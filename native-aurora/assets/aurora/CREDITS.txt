Default video: Blue Bokeh.mp4, supplied by the user.
The bundled aurora/blue-bokeh.mp4 is a 1920x1080/30 H.264 (yuv420p) copy,
transcoded from the supplied 4K AV1 original for broader decoder compatibility.
It has no audio and loops at a default 0.5x (24 seconds per 12-second source loop).
The source file on the user's PC is unchanged. No runtime transcoding occurs.
The user confirmed ownership or permission to redistribute this video
in this public fork and its APK. No additional open artwork license is asserted.
Unsupported playback retains the still wallpaper. Custom MP4s stay local.

RAM display: Android own-process proportional set size (PSS), and device
used RAM (totalMem - availMem), each against total device RAM. Readings are
sampled at approximately five-second intervals and may be cached by Android.
PSS is not Dart heap usage and may exclude decoder/GPU allocations in other
processes. Device usage includes all apps and the OS, not just this launcher.
