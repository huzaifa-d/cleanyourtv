Cherry Blossom theme: Cherry Blossom Background.jpg and Petals.png,
supplied by the user. No additional open artwork license is asserted.
The scheduled defaults derive from background_morning.png, background_night.png,
foreground_morning.png and foreground_night.png in the user-supplied
cherry_blossom_layered_assets.zip. Backgrounds are lossless 3840 x 2160 copies.
The foreground alpha masks are completed around nearby flowers omitted from
the supplied cutouts. Foreground RGB is reconstructed against the background
so both dry scenes remain pixel-identical to the original composites.
The exports are lossless, with no artwork resampling or recolouring.
The original 2000 x 1125 JPEG remains a decode
failure fallback. Detailed Full Moon on Transparent Background.png is also
user-supplied; an inscribed circular crop removes exterior fringe, with a
2-pixel alpha feather before downsampling to a 512 x 512 transparent master.
The runtime rotates the Moon texture 180 degrees without mirroring it.
Original PC files are unchanged. No additional open artwork licence is asserted.
Seven transparent components were extracted from the 1313 x 1198 RGBA sheet,
removing disconnected faint alpha debris only. The blossom content is
649 x 597; the six petal contents are 315-353 pixels wide. Exports retain
native resolution with 12 pixels of transparent padding on each edge.
No upscaling, sharpening, recoloring or inpainting was applied.
The original sheet and source metadata are retained in cherry/source, which
is not staged into the APK. Sixteen native sprites share six petal textures.

RAM display: Android own-process proportional set size (PSS), and device
used RAM (totalMem - availMem), each against total device RAM. Readings are
sampled at approximately five-second intervals and may be cached by Android.
PSS is not Dart heap usage and may exclude decoder/GPU allocations in other
processes. Device usage includes all apps and the OS, not just this launcher.
