The Gradle wrapper, build configuration foundation and Android application
structure are adapted from launcher/ at this revision.
LibraryLoads.java, MemoryTrimPolicy.java and their JVM tests are retained.
AppCatalog and AppLibraryDialog adapt AppLibraryDialog.java's dual-category
query precedence, worker-thread artwork loading, current-generation checks
and recycled-view icon guards. The renderer is changed to Aurora's grid;
upstream home-editing and theme features are not included.
SystemSettings adapts the standard settings-intent resolution. InputsController
adapts MainActivity's TvInputManager discovery and channel-intent construction,
with passthrough and tuner intents separated. Sony package fallbacks come from
the upstream Sony examples; other vendor-specific source mappings are omitted.
No upstream setup, cleanup or package-disabling scripts are executed.

Visual design, artwork and optional private media originate in the Neons
Aurora implementation at 461d9d77d75e36c3febb103d4e87eeb4476ba497.
The imported Aurora implementation retains the GTvLauncher GPLv3 licence in
native-aurora/LICENSE; it is not relicensed under the upstream repository's MIT
licence. The original CleanYourTV launcher and tools remain unchanged under
their existing licence. See native-aurora/assets/aurora/CREDITS.txt for artwork.
